About the Resort

Welcome to The Ambience Resort

The Ambience Resort is a wedding resort and banquet venue on Rajpura–Patiala Road, Punjab. Created for weddings, receptions, family functions, birthday celebrations and corporate gatherings, our elegant indoor halls, celebration lawn and all-season event spaces provide a memorable setting for every occasion.
